Pricing Questions

What is included in the design fee?

Our design fee includes detailed 2D floor plans, photorealistic 3D renderings, material selection guidance, color consultation, and comprehensive site measurements to ensure spatial accuracy.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes, we require an initial retainer fee or deposit to book your project slot and initiate the design phase. The remaining balance is split into milestones tied to project progress.

Can packages be customized?

Absolutely. Every home and client requirement is unique. We offer tailorable design scopes to match either specific rooms or complete turnkey residential transformations.
