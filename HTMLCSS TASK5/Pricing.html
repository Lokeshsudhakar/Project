FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Oppty's platform and services.

What frameworks and languages does Oppty support?

Oppty supports a wide range of modern frameworks and languages out of the box, including Next.js, React, Vue, Node.js, Python, Go, and Docker containers. If your application can be containerized, you can run it on Oppty.

How does pricing work?

Our pricing is designed to be simple and predictable. You pay a flat monthly rate based on your plan tier. If you exceed your plan's included bandwidth or build minutes, we offer transparent, pay-as-you-go overage rates so your site never goes offline.

What is your uptime guarantee?

We offer a 99.99% uptime Service Level Agreement (SLA) for all users on our Business and Enterprise plans. Our infrastructure is distributed globally across multiple availability zones to ensure maximum reliability.

Can I migrate from my current provider?

Absolutely. We provide automated migration tools for common platforms, and our support team offers free, white-glove migration assistance for customers moving to our Business or Enterprise plans.

How do you handle security and compliance?

Security is built into every layer of our platform. We are SOC 2 Type II compliant, offer end-to-end encryption, automated DDoS protection, and detailed audit logs. Enterprise customers can also request custom compliance reports.

Our team can help you find the right plan for your infrastructure needs. Start a conversation today.
