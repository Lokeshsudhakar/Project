About Oppty

Founded in 2021, Oppty is the developer-first cloud platform making infrastructure invisible so teams can focus on shipping.

Our Mission

Making Infrastructure Invisible

"To empower every developer to deploy, scale, and manage applications without worrying about infrastructure complexity."

Oppty was founded in 2021 by a team of engineers who were frustrated with the complexity of existing cloud platforms. We believed that deploying and scaling applications should be as simple as pushing code to Git — no YAML files, no Terraform configs, no operations team required.

Today, Oppty powers over 500,000 deployments across 40+ global regions. From indie developers shipping side projects to enterprise teams running mission-critical workloads, our platform scales to meet every need while keeping the developer experience front and center.

Our Values

The principles that guide how we build and operate Oppty.

Developer First

Every feature we build starts with the developer experience. If it's not intuitive, it's not ready to ship.

Reliability Above All

We obsess over uptime. Our 99.99% SLA isn't just a number — it's a promise backed by engineering rigor.

Open by Default

We contribute to open source, publish our engineering blog, and believe transparency builds trust.
